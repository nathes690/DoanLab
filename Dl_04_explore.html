Research

Current projects

Four active studies, spanning corneal pathogens, uveitis tissue, mass drug distribution, and a global look at conjunctivitis epidemics.

  • capriCORN

    Comprehensive Analysis of Pathogens, Resistomes, and Inflammatory-markers in the CORNea.

  • LOTUS

    Leveraging Ocular Tissues in Uveitis Study — working with partners at home and abroad on how ocular inflammatory disease arises, and on better classification, diagnosis, and biomarkers.

  • MORDOR / AVENIR

    Large randomized trials testing whether mass drug distribution improves childhood survival in Niger and Burkina Faso.

  • SCORPIO

    An international, multi-site study of conjunctivitis epidemics: which pathogens drive them, how their genomes shift across time and place, and how the immune system answers.
